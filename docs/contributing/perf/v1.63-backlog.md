`1.62.0`, grafted into the table when `1.57.0-1.60.0` closed, because
`1.62.0` is the last version before `1.63.0` with a complete measured
set, and its two proof rows are the only cold/warm samples in the phase

at `load 0.74`; pipeline `43.2 ms / 72 ms`; `14 106` warm `newfstatat`). The
`1.63.0` adacovex-side work folds in as a reading note, not as a table column,
because its own cold prove sample cannot yet be separated from load and its
phase role is prove in the next quiet window, not ship a column now.

## What is deferred

### 1.63.0 cold prove at a quieter load

The goal is to give `1.63.0` a real cold sample so it can either become a
column or be documented with a clean own-load figure instead of a note about
being deferred. The runs that produced the `10 544` (load 21) and the
`91.8 s +/- 2.3 s` (load 6.7) samples are not publishable as a column; they
are evidence only that the shape is solver-bound and load-dominated, which
is the old story, and that is why the backlog keeps this item pending.

### 1.62.0 complete set on its own tree state

The `1.62.0` pipeline, syscall, and stripped-size rows used for the
representative come from a fresh worktree checkout of `v1.62.0` (no
`.venv`, no `docs/_build`, no committed `obj/`). The published `1.61.0`
and `1.63.0` rows describe the same shape on this principle. Before
promoting `1.63.0` to a column or re-checking `1.61.0` and `1.62.0`
rows, the three worktrees must be re-measured in the same quiet window so
the three rows share one machine state and one load convention. That is the
deferred piece: it is the cheapest way to make the three rows comparable, but
it is not worth forcing now while the box is shared and the `1.63.0` cold
shape is the one that really needs a quiet load.

### 1.63.0 warm-side admit

The `1.63.0` memo/layout change needs the pages to state, as a choice,
whether it is a phase boundary (open `1.63.0-` at the same time the
`1.61.0-` column is locked to `1.62.0`) or a fold-in inside `1.61.0-`.
The current state says fold-in as a reading note. That is a deliberate call,
not a missing measurement; a future pass decides it when it writes the
`1.63.0` reading note.

## How to finish it

1. Wait for a quiet window (`/proc/loadavg` 1-min below about `3`).
2. Build `v1.61.0`, `v1.62.0`, and current release in three fresh trees
   (no `.venv`, no `docs/_build`, no committed `obj/`), each with its own
   `gnatprove` run so the pipeline rows see each version's own proof summary.
3. Run one interleaved hyperfine session over the three binaries (pipeline
   cold 10, pipeline warm 15, warm strace `newfstatat`, stripped size) so
   one load applies to all three and the rows are comparable.
4. If that window has a quiet cold period, run `prove cold` (3 runs, with
   the result cache and `obj/gnatprove/` wiped) against `1.63.0` and record
   its mean with its load; otherwise keep it deferred.
5. Decide the column shape: if `1.63.0`'s cold sample is clean, open
   `1.63.0-` and lock the `1.61.0-` column to `1.62.0`; if not, keep
   `1.61.0-` as the open phase, `1.62.0` as its representative, and move the
   `1.63.0` memo into a clean reading-note paragraph with its own load.
